Episode 01 // The Signal

Before They Knew Each Other

Luna had been in Veilore for less than a month. She had no childhood memories she could trust, no explanation for the gaps in her past, and no idea why certain places made her feel as though she had already been there.

Every night at exactly 2:17 a.m., an old device in her room came alive with a pulse. No number. No caller. No message. Just a repeating signal that seemed to answer something inside her.

Across town, Dawn heard the same pulse.

She had learned not to ignore patterns. Dawn was patient and observant, the kind of woman who noticed what everyone else dismissed. When the same symbol appeared beside the transmission for the third night in a row, she knew the signal was not random.

“If you're hearing this too, you're not imagining it.”

Neither woman knew the other existed. They certainly did not know that following the signal would bring them together, first as strangers, then as friends, then as something so close it would begin to feel like family.

And neither could know the most dangerous part yet: whatever had brought them together seemed to have rules of its own.

The signal stopped.

For one breath, the room was silent.

Then Luna's screen displayed a single line:

FIND HER BEFORE SHE FINDS YOU.
