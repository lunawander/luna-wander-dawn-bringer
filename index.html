01 // THE STORY

A STRANGER IN A WORLD THAT REMEMBERS

ORIGINAL ART // FRAGMENT 01

Luna arrives with a feeling she cannot explain: somewhere in this unfamiliar place, something is waiting for her. She knows the faces are new, the streets are new, and the rules are new—but the strange signal in her dreams feels older than all of them.

Dawn notices the pattern before anyone else does. She is patient, observant and careful not to decide Luna's story for her. Instead, she helps Luna follow the clues: a broken transmission, a missing archive entry, and a symbol that keeps appearing where it should not.

Their first real lead sends them beyond the safe part of town and onto a road neither of them planned to take. Along the way, friendship becomes devotion, and devotion starts to blur into a strange attraction neither woman dares to name. They begin to feel almost like sisters — inseparable, protective and fiercely loyal — yet something deeper flickers underneath, an unspoken pull that feels beautiful and terrifying at the same time.

“If the world forgot you on purpose, maybe the first thing we need to find is the reason.”

03 // THE FORBIDDEN FEELING

A BOND THAT SHOULD NOT EXIST.

Luna and Dawn were never supposed to meet. One arrived carrying questions; the other already knew that some questions are dangerous to ask.

As Dawn guides Luna through her self-discovery, the strangers become companions, the companions become family, and eventually they become almost impossible to separate. They laugh together. Protect each other. Finish each other's thoughts. When Luna is afraid, Dawn is the person she looks for first.

But beneath that closeness is a strange attraction — a quiet, magnetic feeling neither woman is willing to speak aloud. It feels less like a choice and more like a spell.

And somewhere in the forgotten past, a warning waits: if they surrender to that love, something terrible will awaken. Not only for them, but for everyone they have come to know in this life.
