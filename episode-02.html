Episode 02 // New Roads

The First Choice They Make Together

Dawn had planned to drive Luna as far as the old northern checkpoint, verify the source of the signal, and bring her home before dark. It was a careful plan. Dawn liked careful plans.

Luna ruined it before sunrise.

She fell asleep against the passenger window and woke with a name on her lips: Veridia. She had never heard it before. She could describe its valley, the broken tower on the ridge, even the narrow bridge beneath it.

Dawn knew all of those things were real.

“How do you remember somewhere you've never been?”

For several miles, Dawn avoided answering. Luna did not push. That restraint mattered. Dawn was accustomed to people demanding the pieces of her they wanted; Luna simply waited until she was ready.

Eventually Dawn admitted that Veridia was a place from an old story—one the city archives had quietly removed years ago. According to the legend, two people once found each other there under impossible circumstances. Their connection became so powerful that the world around them began to change.

“Was it love?” Luna asked.

Dawn kept her eyes on the road.

“The story never used that word.”

Neither of them spoke for a while after that.

A Memory That Isn't Hers

When they stopped at an abandoned overlook, Luna recognized everything—the cracked stone railing, the outline of the mountains, even a faded symbol scratched beneath a bench. Her body reacted before her mind could understand why.

A memory struck her: running through purple rain, someone calling her name, and a warm hand refusing to let go.

When the vision vanished, Luna realized she was holding Dawn's hand in the present.

Dawn did not pull away immediately.

“For a second,” Luna whispered, “it felt like I already knew you.”

Dawn's expression softened, then tightened with something closer to fear.

“You don't,” she said. “Not yet.”

The words should have created distance. Instead they made the silence between them feel charged—warm, strange, and far too intimate for two women who had met only days earlier.

The Door at the End of the Road

The coordinates hidden inside the signal led them to an abandoned relay station outside Veridia. The building had no power and no registered owner. Yet when Luna touched the sealed entrance, lights beneath the metal flickered awake.

A message appeared:

PAIR RECOGNIZED. ACCESS REQUIRES BOTH.

Dawn stepped back.

Luna looked at her. “Both of us?”

Before Dawn could answer, a second message burned across the door:

WARNING: DO NOT COMPLETE THE BOND.

The same strange pull passed between them—the attraction neither woman had named, sudden and unmistakable.

Then the door unlocked.

Inside, somewhere in the dark, an archive terminal came online and displayed a file with Luna's full name.

The creation date was twenty-three years before Luna arrived in Veilore.
